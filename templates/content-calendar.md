# Content calendar — [TO FILL: channel name]

The first 30 posts and the 90-day strategy. Every idea carries the same ten facts: audience moment, one-line promise, format, hook, proof or value, making method, packaging idea, effort, risk and success metric. Success metrics name a metric id from channel-metrics.md and are hypotheses, never promised numbers. Status values: idea, planned, in_production, ready, published_by_owner, hold (the harness never publishes; `published_by_owner` is impossible while posts_published is 0).

## Principles
- Pillar mix and cadence are hypotheses to test.
- Effort: S under half a day, M about a day, L several days (adjust to the channel and note it here).
- Production spend is never assumed: a paid step needs its own quote and approval.
