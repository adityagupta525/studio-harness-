# 09 — AI film production pipeline

```
Brief → research pack → treatment → story / character / world bible → shot list
→ per-shot specification → approval to generate → continuity QA → edit
→ asset / rights ledger → delivery
```
Applies to runs whose `practice` is `film` (or film components of a larger run). A run with `practice: unknown` never enters it. Owner: `ai-film-lead` under the Director. Can build on film-prompt (story to finished cut), model-spec (verified before use) and visual-library skills from a private pack listed in the workspace profile.md (if any). This page adds the contract; it does not replace their craft.

## Stages
| # | Stage | Output | Gate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brief | `brief.md` | G1 | context, success measure, credit ceiling |
| 2 | Research pack | `research-pack.md`, `sources.csv` | G2–G3 | 8+ real references, measured |
| 3 | Treatment | `treatment.md` | G6 | derived from the picked route and the client system, not a house style |
| 4 | Story / character / world bible | section 3 of the treatment | G6 | continuity locks every shot inherits |
| 5 | Shot list | beats table + file list | G6 | one row per shot |
| 6 | Per-shot specification | `shots/Sxx.md` | G6 | all required fields (below) |
| 7 | **Approval to generate** | rows in `approvals.csv` | G7 | quote first; one batch per approval |
| 8 | Generation and continuity QA | assets (git-ignored), updated specs | G7 | per-shot checklist |
| 9 | Edit | edit plan, cut, captions, sound | G7 | `motion-editor` |
| 10 | Asset / rights ledger | `rights.csv` | G7–G9 | every asset, incl. generated |
| 11 | Independent QA and delivery | `qa-report.md`, delivered files | G8–G9 | reviewer ≠ producer |

## Shot spec: required fields
Template: [`templates/shot-spec.md`](../templates/shot-spec.md). Front matter, one line per value:

`story_purpose · subject · action · place · camera · lens · movement · composition · lighting · colour_grade · sound · continuity_locks · banned_cliches · reference_evidence · selected_tool · qa_criteria · approval_state`, plus `generation_approval_id` and `shot_id`.

States: `draft` → `approved_to_generate` → `generated` → `qa_passed` (or `rejected`).
- `draft` may carry `[TO FILL]` or `[TO VERIFY]` (warned).
- From `approved_to_generate` on: **no empty field, no `[TO VERIFY]`**, at least one real `reference_evidence` source id, a chosen `selected_tool`, and `generation_approval_id` pointing to an **approved `paid_generation`** row.
- `scripts/check-run-completeness.sh` enforces all of the above.

## Reference rules
- References are analysed for technique, structure, grade and pacing, never copied.
- **Never feed unlicensed third-party reference imagery or footage into a model.** Only `own`, `licensed` or `public-domain` assets with a cleared rights row may be model input. Describe the technique in words instead.
- An AI-made reference is labelled `AI-made`.

## Approval to generate
1. List the shots and tool for this batch; a model-spec skill (from a private pack listed in the workspace profile.md, if any) proposes, a spec check against official pages re-verifies the specs used (within 7 days), Director picks. Model specs, prices and limits are not stored in this repo (`policies/model-routing.yaml`).
2. Get a cost quote (`[V]/[W]/[U]` and date). Write a `pending` `paid_generation` row.
3. The owner approves in chat; record the owner's exact words. Only then generate.
4. After generating, set `executed_on`, `result_ref` (output paths, credits spent).
5. A re-run or an extra shot is a new approval.

## Continuity QA
Per shot, against the bible and its neighbours: subject and wardrobe, light direction and colour, lens and movement per spec, banned clichés absent, rights row present. A batch diagnosis is stated: same failure repeating, high variance, or forgettable. Fixes follow a patch approach (change one thing, record it); do not regenerate blindly, because each attempt may spend credits and needs approval.

## Edit and sound
`motion-editor` builds the edit plan and exports (ffmpeg, Remotion or After Effects once verified). Music: a track's reuse is flagged for the owner's licensing decision; a track or voice generated by a paid tool is also a paid step. Loudness and technical checks are in [05](05-verification-and-qa.md).

## Delivery
- `qa-report.md` result is PASS.
- Every delivered asset is `cleared` in `rights.csv` with a recorded licence decision.
- Platform specs are read from the platform's current official page `[TO VERIFY]` at delivery time.
- Delivery to a client or platform is `external_communication` or `publishing`: its own approval.

## Failure modes this pipeline is built to stop
- A paid shot generated before approval.
- A third-party frame used as a model reference.
- A shot spec with empty lens, light or continuity fields.
- A "fast cut" or "cinematic" claim with no measurement.
- A model spec quoted from memory.
